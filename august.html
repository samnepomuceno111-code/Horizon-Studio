August 15, 2026

Happy 15th Monthsary, my babyy!!


WOAHH, 15 months with youuu. We've already gone through so much in these past months... so many random little memories, happy days, stressful days, and somehow we're still here. Still choosing each other. I know this month wasn't exactly the easiest for us… but I'm really glad that we're here with each other to celebrate this, or even just acknowledge it. Because even that alone makes me happy, babyy. I get to write another one of these for you heh.


I asked you earlier, “hmm baby, what makes me worth staying for?” and you said it's because you genuinely want a future with me and can't imagine a life without me being a part of it. I don't think you know how much that actually meant to me 🥲. So if you asked me the same thing, what makes YOU worth staying for? Well... where do I even start. I don't think I could ever give you one answer because there are just too many little things about you that I've come to love over time. So yesyes, I thought I'd make this month's letter a little appreciation thingy. Fifteen reasons for fifteen months. So baby, get comfy 😇.


First of all, I love how you make me want to keep learning about you. Even after 15 months, I still feel like there are so many things about you that I don't know yet. There are still stories I haven't heard, little thoughts you haven't told me, things that make you happy, things that bother you, random memories from before I even knew you, n so much more. I don't think I'll ever get tired of learning about you. Baby, I want to know the little things too, even the things you think are too insignificant to tell me. I want to keep meeting every version of you as you grow, even the ones I haven't gotten to meet yet. I love the you that I know now, but I know you'll keep changing and growing, and I want to be there for that too. Maybe that's one of the best parts about loving someone. You get to keep discovering them, again and again, in ways you never expected.


Another thing is how you somehow make the most ordinary days worth remembering. Sometimes we're literally just talking about nothing, complaining about school, sending random messages, laughing at something so stupid that prolly wouldn't even be funny to anyone else, getting distracted by the books around us, or just sitting beside each other doing our own thing (ofc we’re still making some physical contact). Yet somehow I still remember those moments. I think that's why even little things like going to the library with you mean so much to me. It's not even really about what we're doing anymore. It's just that it's *you* I'm doing it with. mm yeah, I really do love those little moments with youuu. I think I'd rather have a thousand ordinary days with you than one perfect day without you. Well, would it really be a perfect day if you weren’t a part of it??


I also reallyyy appreciate how comfortable you've made me feel around you. I've always been quite guarded, n you know that. I don't always let people see the softer parts of me, n sometimes it's easier for me to act like I don't care than to actually admit that I do. But with you, I get to be childish. I get to be loud, high, dramatic, clingy, stoopid, and whatever else I become when I'm comfortable. I can say the dumbest things, yap about something completely random, n somehow you still stay. With you, I don't feel like I have to constantly keep myself together. I can just exist. I can breathe. You became a place where I can put down all the walls I carry around with me, and baby, I don't think I'll ever stop being thankful for that.


MOVING ONNN.


Thank you for always trying to understand me. I know I can be difficult at times, especially when I don't even know how to explain what I'm feeling myself. I usually end up telling you anyway but sometimes I can’t help but keep it to myself as well 🥲. So thank you for the patience… it means so much to me, babyy.


Another reason is that you've seen so many versions of me. You've seen me happy, stressed, tired, emotional, frustrated, confused, and probably completely out of brains (yes, plural) at some point . You've seen the parts of me that I'm proud of and the parts I'd rather hide away. We've both made mistakes, we've both had moments where we could've handled things better, and there have definitely been times where things between us weren't easy. But you're still here. And I think there's something really special about being known beyond just the easy version of yourself. You've seen me when I'm not at my best, and you still try to understand me. Thank you for that, babyy.


And then there's the way you care. Sometimes it's really obvious, and sometimes it's in the smallest things that you probably don't even realize I notice. The little questions, the random check-ins, remembering something I said, trying to help me, staying beside me when I'm upset. Like that time in the admin building 🥲 when I was crying and you simply held me in your arms. You didn't need to say anything perfect or fix everything for me. You were just there. I don't think you realize how much those moments mean to me, baby. Sometimes I don't need an answer. I just need you beside me, and somehow you make everything feel a little less heavy.


I also want to remind you of how proud I am of you. I know you don't always see how much you've grown (tho maybe not in height 😇 don't kill me, baby) because you're usually looking at the things you still need to improve, but I see it. I see how much effort you put into things. I see how much you care. I see you trying even when you're tired. You don't have to accomplish something huge every day for me to be proud of you. I'm just proud because you're still trying, still getting back up, still doing your best. And whenever you forget how capable you are, I'll be here to remind you, yesyes. Unfortunately you're stuck with my reminders now.


And OBVIOUSLYY you're worth staying for because you make me laugh and smile. Sometimes I genuinely don't know how you manage to say something so cancel-worthy and somehow I'm already smiling at my phone. I love our random conversations, how one topic somehow turns into five completely unrelated ones, the little jokes (heh, opol) that only make sense to us, n all the moments where we're just being high together. I don't think everything between us needs to be deep or serious all the time. Sometimes I just want to laugh with you. Sometimes I just want to be stoopid with you. Those moments are special too, baby.


Mm, I think one of the biggest reasons is that you make me want to keep trying for us. We've had moments where giving up would've been easier. We've had days where staying felt difficult, days where we weren't sure where we were going, and days where it felt like we were slowly falling apart. But somehow we kept finding our way back to each other. Not because everything suddenly became perfect, but because there was still something here that we both thought was worth taking care of. And I think that's what makes what we have so special to me. We don't only get to love each other when everything is easy. We get to learn how to love each other when things aren't.


I think that's something I've learned from you too. Love isn't always going to be pretty. Sometimes it's communicating when you'd rather shut down. Sometimes it's being patient when you're frustrated. Sometimes it's admitting when you were wrong, listening even when you don't completely understand, and learning how the other person wants to be loved. I know we still have a lot to learn, baby. I definitely do too. But I want to keep learning those things with you. I want to keep becoming better for myself, for you, and for us. I don't want to just say I love you and leave it at that. I want my actions to keep showing you that I do.


And yk how you said earlier that you genuinely want a future with me? I really hope you know that I mean it too. When I say I want a future with you, I don't just mean some vague “yeahhh we'll be together someday” kind of thing. I mean I want the little things. I want to wake up and admire your sleepiness. I want to come home and ask you how your day was and listen to you yap about everythingg that happened. I want to watch crime documentaries with you because I know you love such things. I want to fall asleep hugging you and wake up knowing you're still there. I want to see you accomplish the things you've been dreaming about and be there when you finally get to say, “I finally did it.”


I want to grow with you too. I want to see where life takes us and who we both become. I want us to accomplish our dreams, support each other when things get difficult, and celebrate each other when things finally work out. I want to be someone you can look beside you and think, “yeah, she's here alright.” And I hope you know that when I imagine the future, I don't just imagine the big things. I imagine the quiet ones too. Coming home to you. Cuddling after a long day. Sitting beside you while we both do our own things. Listening to you talk about something you're excited about. You falling asleep on me as I watch a random cartoon. Just being there. I think those little moments are what I want most.


We've already been through so many versions of us. There was the us that was still getting to know each other, the us that was excited and nervous, the us that became official, the us that fought too much, the us that broke apart, the us that missed each other, the us that found our way back, and now this us. It's kinda unbelievable when I really think about it. We aren't the same people we were when we first met, and I don't think we're supposed to be. We've grown. We've changed. We've learned. And somehow, even after all those changes, we're still here beside each other. I think that's something really wow… baby. That despite becoming different versions of ourselves, we still recognize each other.


Maybe the biggest reason you're worth staying for is simply that after everything, it's still you. When I look at you, it's still that “woah, I really love this schizo of mine” feeling. Not because I need you to complete me or because I can't exist without you, but because I genuinely want you in my life. I want you here. I want to keep experiencing things with you. I want to keep learning you, supporting you, ragebaiting you, laughing with you, and being there when things get difficult. I want to keep choosing you because I actually want to, not because I feel like I have to.


So if you ask me again, “What makes me worth staying for?” I don't think I'll ever have one perfect answer. There'll probably be another reason next month, then another one after that, because you're constantly giving me new reasons to appreciate you. I could probably keep writing until I run out of storage and again, brains. Mm, if I had to put everything into one answer? I want you here. I want to know you more. I want to be there for the ordinary days and the important ones. I want to keep making memories with you. I want to keep learning how to love you better. I want more of you in my life, baby.


And yeah, maybe I am also stoopidly in love with you 😇. You reached places in my heart that I didn't even know were waiting for someone. You somehow made all these little spaces feel warm n just goodgood, and now I can't imagine them without you there.


Thank you for staying, babyy. Thank you for trying. Thank you for being patient with me, for understanding me, for asking when something's wrong, for making me laugh, for letting me be soft around you, for every hug, every conversation, every random moment, every little effort, and every time you've simply chosen to be there.


I don't need one big reason to love you, baby. I just do... I love you because you're you. I love you through the fun days, the stressful ones, the uncertain ones, and all the seemingly boring little days in between. I love you loudly, quietly, in ways I know how to express and in ways I probably still haven't learned yet.


Happy 15th Monthsary, my babyy !! I LOVE YOUU SOOOOO SOOOOOOO VV MUCHHH. I really hope you know that 🥲. Fifteen months with you, and I still want more. More months, more years, more memories, more weird ahh conversations, more random library trips, more hugs, more learning, more us. I want to see you succeed. I want to see you become everything you want to be. I want to grow beside you while I figure myself out too. So baby, let’s figure life out together.
